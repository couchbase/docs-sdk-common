[TIP]
.No Load Balancer Required
====
Your Couchbase cluster will take care of balancing the load and distribution of data between nodes,
in tandem with the SDK's use of the cluster map.
A load balancer would negatively affect this work -- 
so the use of a load balancer between your app server, where you're running the SDK and client code, and your Couchbase operational cluster, 
is strongly discouraged.
====
